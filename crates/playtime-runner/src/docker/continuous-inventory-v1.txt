The locked workshop provides Python, Bash, Git, ripgrep, standard Unix tools,
NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Pillow, Playwright, Chromium, C/C++, Make, Node.js, npm
and SQLite. Exact installed versions are listed in /opt/workshop/python-packages.txt
and /opt/workshop/system-packages.txt. Installed collections are listed in
/opt/workshop/toolsets.txt. There is no external network.

/work is the bounded writable workspace. Seed inputs, when supplied, are in
/work/seeds. /tmp is temporary storage. Background processes can run across
conversation compactions; redirect their output to files. All processes stop at
the end of the run. Only the final stopped workspace is captured.

read_file accepts UTF-8 text. Use exec for binary metadata or numerical checks;
the model interface does not provide image or audio perception. Images and audio
saved in the workspace are artifacts for the human reviewer.
