An optional collection of small starting materials. You may use, change, or
ignore any of them. These are synthetic examples created for this workshop.

pulses.json: A small deterministic sequence produced by a mixture of periodic
signals. Units are arbitrary; these are not measurements of a physical system.

palette.json: A handful of colors that can be used as raw material.

machine.py: A runnable one-dimensional cellular automaton with a configurable
rule. Run `python machine.py --help` to see its controls.
